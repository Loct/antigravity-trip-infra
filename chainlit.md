# 🌍 Wanderlog Travel Assistant

Welcome to your AI-powered Travel Assistant!

### Quick Commands:
- `/trips` - View your active Wanderlog itineraries
- `/hotel <city> [check-in] [check-out]` - Search Booking.com accommodations with Genius discounts
- `/agoda <city> [check-in] [check-out]` - Search Agoda accommodations
- `/council <question>` - Consult the Anthropic Claude & DeepSeek AI Travel Council
- `/status` - Check connectivity and credentials for all travel services
- `/help` - View complete help options
